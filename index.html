🧑‍💻 About Me

As a seasoned ML & NLP Engineer with experience at MBZUAI and Microsoft, I specialise in developing and deploying large language models (LLMs) and generative AI solutions. My work bridges cutting-edge research and practical applications, focusing on cultural AI and scalable NLP systems. Currently, I work as a Senior Research Engineer at MBZUAI, where I lead initiatives in multilingual LLMs and generative music models, with a focus on representation fairness and low-resource language modelling.

My recent work includes co-developing Nanda, a 10B-parameter Hindi LLM built on LLaMA-3, achieving significant gains (+8%) on MMLU and other benchmark tasks. My experience spans both core ML engineering (Azure, PyTorch, inference optimisation) and research contributions to low-resource data.
